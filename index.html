Military News Bot

A Discord bot that watches military, defense and geopolitics RSS feeds, sorts each article by topic, and drops it into the right channel as a rich embed. Built for OSINT and news communities that want structure instead of noise.

  • Automatic RSS monitoring with keyword-based routing
  • Flash alerts for high-priority breaking news
  • SQLite duplicate protection and scheduled cleanup
